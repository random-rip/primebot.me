## Privacy policy of PrimeBot on Discord and Telegram

PrimeBot (hereinafter referred to as "Bot") stores the following information:

- Teams registered on Telegram: Telegram group ID
- Teams registered on Discord: server ID, channel ID, channel name, webhook ID, webhook token, Discord role
- Team information, such as the name or the personalization of notifications. You can find the Prime League's privacy policy [here](https://docs.gamesports.net/privacy/en).

If you want this data deleted at any time, you can run the `/delete` command.

## Privacy policy of the PrimeBot website

1. Data and information of visitors and users (date, time, page visited) may be stored when the server is accessed. We point out that no personal data (e.g. name, address or email address) is stored without consent.
2. Should personal data be collected, we undertake to obtain the prior consent of the website user. We undertake not to pass on any data to third parties unless the visitor or user consents beforehand.
3. We point out that data transmission over the internet (e.g. via email) may have security gaps. Therefore, error-free and uninterrupted protection of third-party data cannot be fully guaranteed. Our liability in this regard is excluded.
4. You have the right to information. You have the right to receive complete information about the data stored about you from us, free of charge, at any time.
5. Furthermore, users have the right to rectification/erasure of data and to restriction of processing.
