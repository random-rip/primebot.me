::u-page-section{id="features" title="Features"}
#description
PrimeBot sends you **notifications about every change**, straight into your Discord channels or Telegram groups. The Prime League is the largest league for all League of Legends players in the DACH region.

#features
  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-calendar-plus
  title: New scheduling suggestions
  description: When your opponents suggest new dates, you get all suggested times along with links to the match day and the opposing team. Your own suggestions too, if you like.
  ---
  :::

  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-calendar-check
  title: Scheduling confirmations
  description: As soon as a date has been accepted, PrimeBot sends you a confirmation with the agreed date and further links.
  ---
  :::

  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-users
  title: New lineups of your opponents
  description: You get notified as soon as an opposing team sets up a (new) lineup, with an op.gg or u.gg link to all players.
  ---
  :::

  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-message-square
  title: New comments
  description: New comments on your matches land right with you. Comments by your own team members are excluded.
  ---
  :::

  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-swords
  title: Detailed match info
  description: With /match you get the scheduling status, scouting links, both lineups, banned champions and side selection at a glance.
  ---
  :::

  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-calendar-days
  title: Weekly overview
  description: At the start of every match week you get all open games with dates and lineup links. Available anytime via /matches.
  ---
  :::

  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-sliders-horizontal
  title: Personalization
  description: Customize your notifications, switch to German, pick op.gg, u.gg or xdx.gg as your scouting website and have a role mentioned in Discord via /role.
  ---
  :::

  :::u-page-feature
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-clock
  title: Within 15 minutes
  description: PrimeBot checks the Prime League for news regularly, notifications arrive after about 15 minutes at the latest.
  ---
  :::
::
